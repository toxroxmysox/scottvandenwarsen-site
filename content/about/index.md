I wanted a space on the internet that could be flexible and entirely my own. This site serves as a place to share photos of my travels and adventures, recommend interesting articles, podcasts, and other things I find worth sharing, and share and connect without the pressure of follower counts or engagement metrics. You might find an article about recent technology, a gallery from a recent trip, or my thoughts on the surprising overlap between a dungeon master for a D&D session and a facilitator for a professional workshop.

As a University of Michigan alumnus based in Detroit, my wife and I travel one glass of wine or cup of coffee at a time. One day I might convince her to bring our two cats along, though that may take a while. If you want to follow along, you can subscribe for updates on new galleries and posts.

I'm an engineer focused on improving systems to make my life easier, building meaningful and intentional relationships, and reading far too many product reviews before deciding on what to buy. Take a look around and see if anything here resonates!
