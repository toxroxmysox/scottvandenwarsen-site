This was a really fascinating listen about a man in San Francisco and it’s not often we get such an inside look at a subject normally so taboo. I learned I don’t really know that much about homelessness or drug addition and this was a very personal story with someone opening up about their life. I highly recommend a listen to the 3-part series digging into his challenges, San Francisco’s public services, and his journey with recovery.
