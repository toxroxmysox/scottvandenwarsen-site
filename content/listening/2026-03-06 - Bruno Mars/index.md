This is the first time I've listened to a full album from Bruno Mars instead of his hyper-popular hits, but this album works for me and is a great vibe. It feels like a fun mix of pop and soul/funk and I can't turn it off. This gives off a similar feeling that country music does, where it automatically puts me in a good mood and wanting to dance and sing along.
