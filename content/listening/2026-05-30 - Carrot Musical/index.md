When I downloaded the Carrot Weather app as a replacement for Dark Sky, I just liked the fact it was a bit snarky and a bit different from other weather apps that just tell you all the same stuff. I didn't realize how different is really was. When you keep logging into the app, you sometimes "unlock" a new song, a popup appears, and it's usually swiped away without another thought as with other random notifications. The first time I did listen to the song I unlocked, it had no right to be so good. 

For the weather app's 10th anniversary, they created a musical about the weather app turning sentient and fighting back against the "maker" and the "meatbags" (people) who use the app. There's like 12 songs, and they're equal parts funny and high quality. I honestly didn't know it was made by AI until I looked up the backstory and then I felt a bit disappointed. 

I know it's a weird recommendation but if you like musicals, especially some of the popular ones, I encourage you to give it a listen!
