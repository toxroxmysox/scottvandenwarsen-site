My trip to Chengdu, China in 2024 was my first time in Asia and I made the most of it with a weekend hiking trip to the nearby mountains and a visit to the panda conservatory. Chengdu has the highest density of pandas in the world, and people come from all over! Looking forward to more trips to China to see the big dumb bears and other sights.
