This trip to Egypt was the first family travel experience with all six of us together and it was incredible. Cairo and the Nile have so much Egyptian history, incredible monuments, and delicious food. We did a few days in Cairo to start the trip, saw the pyramids (incredible), rode some camels, and explored the alleys and mosques of the city. The second half of the trip was a cruise down the nile in a dahabiya (small river boat) which was a really fun way to visit all the temples and sites along the Nile - much better than a larger cruise ship, in our opinion, and I'd recommend you do that if you visit.

We did a full trip travel tour company that helped book hotels, guides, transports, and cruises and it made us (and my mom specifically) much more comfortable. It was very nice not to have to worry about the small details in a country different from the US in many ways; it didn't stop us (read: Mark and Sam) from getting scammed on spices or perfumes, and I think they're still going through the saffron they purchased more than two years later.
