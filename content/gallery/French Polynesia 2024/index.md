After our wedding in September of 2024, we spent some time on the island of Moorea in French Polynesia! An incredibly stunning island, clear waters and great food, it was the perfect start to our honeymoon. The island is small enough to drive around and has some picturesque mountains and bays scattered around to visit. We spent some time on a jet ski exploring the reef and took a small boat out to swim with humpback whales during their migration!
