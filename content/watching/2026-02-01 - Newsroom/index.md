I remember watching the NBC Nightly News almost every night when I was growing up because that’s what my dad wanted on the TV and that’s how he got his news and updates about the world. Today, I feel many people my age don’t have or don’t care about cable and choose to get their news from other sources, usually social media like Reddit or Instagram but hopefully at least an official news website. 

The Newsroom is a very optimistic view at what a nightly news broadcast could be and I wish there was something actually like it today. I think you can find pockets of reputable sources that care enough to report facts but sometimes they aren’t big enough to make a dent in the masses. If you find a good news source that you trust, let me know. I am a big fan of tech and I read a lot from [The Verge ](https://www.theverge.com/)and their ethics policy gives me some confidence in their work, but there are a lot of subjects not related to technology.

I think morning newsletters or morning podcasts are more popular with younger people and I see those in my sphere a lot. I read the [Morning Brew](https://www.morningbrew.com/), but I know there are others like the [Skimm](https://www.theskimm.com/news) or [NYT’s The Daily](https://www.nytimes.com/column/the-daily) that offer their top stories and it’s been helpful for me to stay informed without having to spend an hour of my night.
