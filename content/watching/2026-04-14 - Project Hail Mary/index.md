I blitzed through the audiobook of Project Hail Mary because I heard the movie was coming out - usually this doesn't end well, but it represented it pretty well! The book had a ton of science and the movie simplified it to the key parts, and I think people unfamiliar with science could follow along, which is good. 

If you liked The Martian, the other book/movie from Andy Weir, you'll love this. The sound and visuals of the movie alone are worth a night out to the theater, in my book.
