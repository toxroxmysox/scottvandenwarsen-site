I first saw this series on a random tiktok video and couldn't be more excited to have another meaty series to explore. I've been looking for a series that hooked me as much as Stormlight Archive or Mistborn and I found it with Red Rising. The pace is incredible and I was genuinely shocked at a few of the twists and turns throughout the three books I've read so far. Character development is really well done, I can see the growth in just a few books and gets me excited for what's to come. Strong fantasy background with some science fiction mixed in hits a niche for me that I love and I can't wait to dig into the remaining books as the final volume is coming out later in 2026.
